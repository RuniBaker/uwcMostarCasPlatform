About UWC Mostar CAS

What is CAS?

Creativity, Activity, and Service (CAS) is one of the three essential elements of the International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme. Through CAS, students develop skills, attitudes and dispositions through a variety of individual and group experiences that provide students with opportunities to explore their interests and express their passions, personalities and perspectives.

CAS complements a challenging academic programme with a range of experiences that extends students' learning beyond the classroom and helps them develop as unique individuals.

UWC Mostar's Approach

At UWC Mostar, CAS is integrated into our mission of making education a force to unite people, nations and cultures for peace and a sustainable future. Our students engage in meaningful activities that combine creativity, physical activity and service to the community.

The CAS Tracking System helps students and CAS leaders monitor participation, track progress, and ensure all IB requirements are met while fostering personal growth and community engagement.

CAS Components

Creativity

Exploring and extending ideas leading to an original or interpretive product or performance.

Activity

Physical exertion contributing to a healthy lifestyle, complementing academic work.

Service

Collaborative, reciprocal engagement with the community in response to an authentic need.
